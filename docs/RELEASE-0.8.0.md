# FoodCost 0.8.0

The calculator keeps its graphical food-cost dashboard and adds a clearer form below it: food name,
category selector, portions, sale price, optional collapsible discount, and aligned ingredient inputs.
Ingredients and dishes have searchable cards, useful summaries, and working list filters.

Price status is shown with both text and color: suitable is green, near the target is warm cream,
and substantially underpriced is red. Incomplete or unverified figures remain clearly identified.

Settings has a navigable overview with separate pricing, menu sections, costs and online sales,
currency, backup, and help pages. Each editable section saves independently. Backup recovery stays
accessible when the app needs a recovery decision.

General and menu-section pricing are mutually exclusive saved modes. Switching modes preserves
the inactive rules. General mode applies the general rule to every dish. Menu-section mode keeps
legacy recipe overrides, then section rules, with the general rule as fallback. Existing records
without a mode keep their historical behavior. The previous 34-digit percentage conversions,
unconfirmed-percentage checks, and near-100% margin protections are retained.

Currency selection offers toman and rial. Older new-rial drafts and backups remain readable and
convert using their original units. The calculator asks for confirmation before leaving the app.

Signed APKs continue to use the existing application identity and signing certificate.
